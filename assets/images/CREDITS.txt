HEARTHSIDE COMFORT CO. — IMAGE CREDITS
======================================

Every photograph on this site comes from Pexels or Unsplash. Both licences
permit commercial use and neither requires attribution. We credit each file
anyway, and link to the source page where the photographer is named.

Most photographs are from Pexels. Twelve are from Unsplash: partway through the
build Pexels' image CDN began refusing requests from this address, and rather
than ship a page with a missing picture those images were sourced from Unsplash
instead. Each entry below states which licence applies to that file.

We do NOT use images from Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Etsy, Temu, manufacturer
websites, or image search results. No photograph on this site shows a
third-party brand logo.

Photographs are illustrative stock photography of the type of item described.
They are not photographs of the individual unit a customer will receive. Where
no free-licence photograph honestly showed a listed item, the item was replaced
rather than mismatched — see the swap table in catalog.md.
