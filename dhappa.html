Six departments

  • Easy-On Footwear

    12 items. Shoes, slippers and socks you can get on while seated, without bending far or pulling hard.

  • Adaptive Clothing

    10 items. Everyday clothes built around easier closures — magnets, wide plackets, pull-on waists. Clothing first, always.

  • Sleep & Lounge

    10 items. Soft layers for the evening and the morning, cut loose enough to move in and warm enough to sit still in.

  • Warm & Cozy

    12 items. Throws, knitwear and hand-warmers for the chair by the window and the walk to the mailbox.

  • Rest & Relaxation

    8 items. Cushions and pillows that change the shape of a chair or a bed you already own.

  • Everyday Accessories

    4 items. A few small things chosen for large, readable faces, light weight and simple catches.

What we will not do

We do not show star ratings or customer quotes until verified customers have actually written them. We do not print crossed-out “was” prices, because this store has no price history to compare against. We do not tell you that stock is running out, and we will never say that a product treats or relieves any condition.

If you ever see any of those things on this site, we have made a mistake and we would like to know. Write to help@hearthsidecomfort.com.
